Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy describes information processed when you use Neon Block Run, including optional online leaderboard accounts and advertising.

1. Information processed

On your device, Neon Block Run stores gameplay preferences, local scores and progress. If you create an optional online account, the leaderboard service processes your chosen username, account authentication information, session information, and submitted game results such as scores, levels and coins. Technical information such as IP addresses and request or error logs may also be processed by hosting services.

2. How information is used

We use game and account information to provide gameplay features, sign players in, maintain rankings, validate scores, investigate technical problems, secure the service and prevent abuse. Usernames and scores may be publicly visible on leaderboards.

3. Advertising

The Android app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including optional rewarded advertisements, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices where applicable. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, ad interactions and technical or diagnostic information to provide advertising, measure performance and prevent fraud. For further information see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing and service providers

We use third-party services including Railway to host leaderboard functionality and Google to deliver ads and manage applicable consent. These providers may process information to provide their services. Other players can see public leaderboard usernames and scores. We do not sell leaderboard account information.

5. Security

The online leaderboard uses encrypted HTTPS connections. We take reasonable steps to protect account information, but no online service is completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Online account information and associated leaderboard results are held while needed to provide your account and related functionality. You may request account deletion in the game through Global Leaderboard → Account Settings → Delete Account or through our account deletion page even if you have uninstalled the game. The account deletion process is designed to remove the account, its sessions and associated run records from the active leaderboard system. Information in operational logs or backups, where applicable, may remain temporarily under hosting and security retention practices. You may contact us to ask about remaining data relating to a deletion request.

Local gameplay records are stored on your device and can be removed by clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it; deleting the online account may also clear local game progress on the device used for deletion.

7. Children and parents

If you are a parent or guardian and are concerned that a child has shared information through Neon Block Run, contact us using the address below. We will review requests to remove information and respond as appropriate.

8. Contact

Privacy questions, access inquiries and deletion requests: neonrunner2026@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when game features, service providers, or legal obligations change. Any revised policy will appear here with a new effective date.
