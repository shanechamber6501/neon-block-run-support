Delete your Neon Block Run account

You can request deletion of your optional online leaderboard account and its associated data even if you no longer have the game installed.

Option 1 — Delete inside the game

Open Neon Block Run → Global Leaderboard → Account Settings → Delete Account. Re-enter your current account password, type DELETE, and confirm. This is intended to permanently remove the account and associated leaderboard records.

Option 2 — Delete without the game

Use the online account-deletion service. You may need your existing account credentials to confirm ownership.

Open online deletion page

Option 3 — Email a deletion request

If you cannot sign in or do not have access to the app, email us with your leaderboard username and ask for account deletion. We may request information to verify ownership. Never email your password, login tokens or verification codes.

Email a deletion request

neonrunner2026@gmail.com

What data will be deleted?

Account deletion is designed to remove your online username/account, active sign-in sessions and linked leaderboard run records from the active service. Local gameplay data on other devices must be cleared separately, such as by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Operational logs or backup data, where applicable, may remain temporarily under applicable hosting and security retention practices. For information about your request, email support.
